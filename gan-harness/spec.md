# Design Brief: Core-2 Operations Platform UI Enhancement

## Objective
Transform the existing Operations Dashboard (Core-2) into a visually stunning, award-winning interface. The goal is to escape the "generic SaaS dashboard" look and introduce a highly refined, premium, and distinctive visual experience.

## Primary Directives
1. **Visual Excellence over Feature Completeness**: A stunning half-finished app beats a functional ugly one. Push for creative leaps.
2. **Original Layouts & Data Presentation**: Do not default to a grid of identical white cards. Find novel ways to present the operations data (Schedules, Resource Pressure, Approval Queues) using scale, overlapping elements, or unique container shapes.
3. **Typography & Hierarchy**: Use bold, dramatic typographic scales for primary metrics and extremely restrained, elegant secondary text.
4. **Motion & Micro-interactions**: Every interactive state should feel premium. Introduce subtle entrance animations, fluid layout transitions, and tactile hover states.
5. **Color Strategy**: Move beyond the safe canvas/white theme. Use saturated accents deliberately, or explore a deeply immersive dark mode, or a radically restrained monochrome palette with one high-chroma accent.

## Target Areas
- `app-shell.tsx` (Sidebar and top navigation)
- The main Operations Overview view (Cards, lists, progress bars)
- Form controls and interactive elements
